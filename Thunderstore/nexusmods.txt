[font=Arial Black][color=DarkOrange][size=5][b]Enemy Health Bars[/b][/size][/color][/font]

Standalone health bars for enemies in SULFUR. Hit an enemy and a bar appears above it, fades after a few seconds, and goes away when the enemy dies.

The mod does not change damage, enemy stats, or loot. It only reads what the game already knows and draws it.

[color=RoyalBlue][b]Bar styles[/b][/color]

Five looks, all shown at the moment an enemy takes a hit: the red part is the health left, and the pale part is the health just lost.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ryuka-dev/Enemy-Health-Bars/main/docs/styles-default.png[/img]

Sulfur and Rounds follow the game's UI color, so they change with the HUD in areas like the desert.

[color=RoyalBlue][b]How it behaves[/b][/color]

[list]
[*]A bar shows after an enemy loses health and fades about six seconds after the last hit.[/*]
[*]You need a clear line of sight. Enemies behind walls have no bar.[/*]
[*]Enemies the game is hiding stay hidden: invisible enemies, disguised ambushers, and ghosts while they are faded out.[/*]
[*]Bosses keep the game's own boss bar and get no second one.[/*]
[*]Allies, charmed enemies, shopkeepers and other friendly characters have no bar.[/*]
[*]Bars disappear in menus, the inventory, dialogue, cutscenes and on the death screen.[/*]
[*]Enemy names use the game's own translations.[/*]
[/list]
[color=RoyalBlue][b]Settings[/b][/color]

Change these in [color=#ffff00]BepInEx/config/ryuka.sulfur.enemy_health_bars.cfg[/color], or in game through SULFUR Config if you have it installed.

[color=DodgerBlue][b]Display mode[/b][/color]

[code][Display]
DisplayMode = Damaged[/code]
[b]Damaged[/b] shows bars only on enemies that lost health. [b]Nearby[/b] shows bars on every enemy you can see in range, up to 32 at once, nearest first.

[color=DodgerBlue][b]Bar style[/b][/color]

[code][Display]
BarStyle = Sulfur[/code]
[b]Sulfur[/b] matches the game's own health bar and follows its UI color. [b]Rounds[/b] is a row of ten rings like the ammo gauge. [b]Ichor[/b] is a run of dripping ink. [b]Bone[/b] has a skull at the head of the bar. [b]Classic[/b] is a plain bar.

[color=DodgerBlue][b]Max distance[/b][/color]

[code][Display]
MaxDistance = 30[/code]
Farthest distance in meters at which a bar is shown (5 to 100).

[color=DodgerBlue][b]Bar scale[/b][/color]

[code][Display]
BarScale = 1[/code]
Size of the bars and names (0.5 to 2).

[color=DodgerBlue][b]Show name[/b][/color]

[code][Display]
ShowName = true[/code]
Shows the enemy's name above its bar.

[color=RoyalBlue][b]Compatibility[/b][/color]

[list]
[*]Needs only BepInEx. SULFUR Config is optional.[/*]
[*][b]Battle Improvements[/b] has its own enemy health bar. If you run both, turn off its health bar ([color=#ffff00]EnableHealthBar[/color]) or each enemy shows two bars.[/*]
[*]Works alongside Damage Sparks.[/*]
[*]SULFUR Together: everything is local to your game. Bars follow the health the host sends, so on a joining player they can lag slightly behind. Enemies that are invisible until they notice you may not get a bar on a joining player.[/*]
[/list]

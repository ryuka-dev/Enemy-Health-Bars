# Changelog

## 0.2.0

* SULFUR Together team marks now show on the bars, one for each teammate who marked the enemy. Together hides its own icon over that enemy while the bar is up. Needs SULFUR Together 1.8.0 or newer.
* Each bar style draws the marks its own way (dog tags on Sulfur, cartridges on Rounds, and so on).
* If you don't play with SULFUR Together, nothing changes.

## 0.1.0

First release.

* Health bars over enemies that have been hit, with names in your game language.
* A Nearby mode that shows bars on every enemy you can see in range.
* Five bar styles. The default matches the HUD health bar and follows its color.
* No bars through walls, and hidden enemies stay hidden.
